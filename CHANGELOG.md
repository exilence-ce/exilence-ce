# Changelog

## 2.0.0-beta.1

First beta of Exilence CE 2.0, the successor of Exilence CE 1.x, rebuilt as a native desktop app written in Rust.

- Windows installer, one macOS `.dmg` for Apple silicon and Intel, and a Linux AppImage for glibc 2.39 or newer. The AppImage adds Exilence CE with its icon to your app menu and launchers when it starts, and keeps the entry pointing at wherever you moved it.
- Updates download in the background; "Update available, click here to update now!" in the title bar installs them. Choose **Latest Stable** or **Beta** under **Settings → Advanced → Release channel**.
- Settings in tabs: **Net worth** (close action, auto snapshotting, pricing options that apply from the next Snapshot), **Interface** (interface scale 50–150 %, applied at once), **Custom prices** and **Advanced** (release channel; hardware acceleration on Windows and Linux, which now shows its real value). **Reset data** deletes all Exilence CE data on this computer except the logs, signs you out and restarts; legacy Exilence CE data is never touched.
- Your Path of Exile login renews itself: the app refreshes the token before it expires, so you only log in again when a login is older than 90 days or Path of Exile refuses it ("Session expired, please login again").
- Signing out removes your login from this computer and the Exilence server. Path of Exile itself keeps Exilence CE's access until it expires (at most 90 days): to end it at once, remove Exilence CE under **My Account → Applications** on pathofexile.com.
- The app keeps working when the Exilence server is unreachable: outside a Group you never see its state, and it reconnects silently in the background. If your account starts on another computer, this one stops talking to the server until you restart it and tells you so in the notifications.
- When loading your characters fails at startup, a message shows and the app tries again every 45 seconds.
- Profiles: create, edit, switch and remove them from the toolbar. The pricing leagues are the leagues poe.ninja prices, so you can no longer pick one without prices. Profile changes need the Exilence server; when it cannot be reached, the change fails with a message. On your first login the app creates `profile 1` with the first two stash tabs of your first league.
- Prices of your Profile's pricing league load at startup and when you switch to a Profile with another pricing league, and refresh once they are an hour old (not while a Snapshot runs). poe.ninja is asked only for what changed since the last load. If a refresh fails or poe.ninja suddenly has no prices for the league, the previous prices stay. The Net worth view shows how long ago prices were fetched; the toolbar warns when poe.ninja has no prices for the league.
- **Settings → Custom prices** shows the price table of any pricing league with a name search, 25 prices per page. Set a custom price (prefilled with the poe.ninja price) or remove it; custom prices are kept per pricing league on this computer, must be 0 or more, and apply from the next Snapshot when the poe.ninja price passes your pricing options. Custom prices for Exalted Orb and Divine Orb change the conversion rate at once.
- **Take snapshot** in the toolbar fetches your Profile's stash tabs (a selected tab's child tabs count under it) and your character's inventory and/or equipment as a "Character" tab, prices them and saves the Snapshot on this computer, also when the Exilence server is unreachable. The status shows "Fetching stash tab i / N" with an info hint naming GGG's current request allowance; **Cancel** stops it without saving anything until it is being saved. It is enabled once the session, the league's stash tabs and prices have loaded and no rate-limit wait runs. Item detail is kept for the newest 10 Snapshots of a Profile, at most 1000 Snapshots are kept. The Net worth view shows the newest total with its change, the Snapshot count (`1000+` at the cap) and the time since the last Snapshot.
- Net worth widgets: **Net worth** with its change against the previous Snapshot (green or red), **Income** per hour from the Snapshots of the last hour or since you pressed **Reset** (not kept when the app restarts), and the Snapshot count with the time since the last Snapshot. The switch next to the net worth cycles values between exalted, divine and chaos (`ex` / `div` / `c`), applies at once and is remembered; its tooltip shows the rates of your pricing league, custom prices included. Values show 2 decimals in your system's number format, positive changes with `+ `, and `0` while a rate is missing.
- Groups: the **Groups** button in the toolbar opens the Group drawer (and closes the navigation). **Create group** suggests a random name (roll the dice for another) and **Join group** takes an existing one, names matching regardless of case; when the name field loses focus it says whether the name is taken or not found. The password is optional and at least 6 characters; a wrong one shows on the password field. On joining, every player is selected and each player's latest Snapshot counts at once, also one taken before you joined; players who join later appear unselected. Tick a player's checkbox to choose who counts: Net worth, its change, the sparkline, the Snapshot count and the income (over the last hour, no Reset in a Group) combine the selected players, and new Snapshots of the others show up without any action. The net worth chart draws one line per selected player, named by account, within the chosen time span; the tab chart stays your own. The item table merges the selected players' newest Snapshots into one table without the Tabs column (no tab filter in a Group), and its Comparison view compares each player's own two newest Snapshots, merged into one table. When another player switches Profile, their newest Snapshot of that Profile shows at once; edits of their active Profile reach you too. **Leave group** asks first and removes you from the others' views at once. A restarted app rejoins its Group.
- A Group survives connection drops: when the server goes silent for 75 seconds or the connection breaks, the toolbar shows "Not connected to server" and the app reconnects on its own, retrying at most 60 seconds apart. Back within 5 minutes, you rejoin with your player choices kept and only the Snapshots you missed are fetched; a server restart or upgrade does not count against those 5 minutes. After 5 minutes away you have left the Group (noted in the notifications) and continue solo; a Group ends once nobody has been connected for 5 minutes. A Snapshot whose upload failed while in a Group is uploaded again on its own, also right after a reconnect. When your account connects on another computer, this one closes the Group drawer, notes it in the notifications and stays offline until restarted; the other computer continues in the Group.
- Charts on the Net worth view, in sections you can collapse (remembered, collapsed at first): the **Net worth history chart** of your Profile over 1 day, 1 week, 1 month or all time (remembered, default all time), and the **Tab breakdown history chart** with one line per selected stash tab over the newest 50 Snapshots; click a legend entry to hide or show a tab. Hovering shows the nearest Snapshot's local date and time and its value in chaos with 2 decimals; drag across a chart to zoom in, **Reset zoom** to go back. Lines run straight between Snapshots and nothing animates. The Net worth widget shows a sparkline of the newest 10 Snapshots in the same style.
- Auto snapshotting (**Settings → Net worth**): when on, a Snapshot is taken right after the app has started your session and right after a Group reconnect, and the next one one interval after each Snapshot finishes, fails or is cancelled. Turning it on or changing the interval schedules the next one an interval later. When a Snapshot cannot start at that time, it tries again every 10 seconds, also when the Exilence server is unreachable.
- The item table on the Net worth view lists the items of your newest Snapshot, the same item across tabs merged into one row: icon, name in its rarity colour with a link to its poe.ninja page, item level or map tier, tabs, corrupted, links, quality, level, quantity, the price trend of the last 7 days (sparkline and %), price, total value and the cumulative value. The trend and the poe.ninja link come from the current prices of the Snapshot's pricing league, so older Snapshots show today's trend. Click a header to sort, drag a header's edge to resize, hide columns under **Edit columns**, and page through 5, 10, 25 or 50 rows; sort, widths, rows per page and hidden columns are remembered. Switching Profile goes back to the first page.
- Item table tools: search items (up to 20 characters) by name, tab name, rarity (`normal`, `magic`, `rare`, `unique`, `gem`, `currency`, `divination`, `quest`, `unknown`, `legacy`) or a regular expression, ignoring case; **Create filter** narrows the rows to the stash tabs you tick; while either is in use the filter total shows in your display currency. **Edit columns** offers legacy's column presets (Custom, Breachstones, Catalysts, …); the chosen preset and its columns are remembered. **Export data** saves the rows you see as a CSV file (`Export_YYYY-MM-DD-HH-mm.csv`, titled `Data from YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm`, with a UTF-8 BOM so Excel reads it). The comparison view shows what changed between your two newest Snapshots: new items, changed quantities as the difference and vanished items negated, gains with `+ ` in green, losses in red; a Snapshot without items (older than the newest 10, or imported) counts as empty.
- **Remove snapshots** in the toolbar lists your Profile's Snapshots as `Snapshot YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm, value: X.XXc`, newest first, with a warning mark on those without items (older than the newest 10, or imported); select some or all and remove them. Removing works also when the Exilence server cannot be reached: the Snapshots are gone from this computer at once, and the server deletes its copies as soon as it can be reached again.
- Diagnostics instead of error reporting: the app always keeps a log (rotated, 5 × 10 MB). **Settings → Advanced → Debug mode** (or starting with `--debug` or the `EXILENCE_LOG` environment variable) logs every request to Path of Exile, poe.ninja and the Exilence server in detail, applies at once and shows DEBUG in the toolbar. **Export diagnostics** (Settings → Advanced and the support panel) saves a zip with the logs, versions, system details, settings and rate-limit state for a bug report; nothing is uploaded, and tokens, login codes and passwords never appear in a log or the zip. **Open log folder** opens the logs. After a crash, the next start notes it in the notifications.
- Bring your Exilence CE history along: after your first login on a computer that has Exilence CE (or Exilence Next) data, a dialog lists its accounts with their Profiles, snapshot counts and dates, your own account names ticked. **Import** brings the ticked accounts' Profiles with every snapshot's net worth and tab values (no item detail; the item table fills with your next snapshot), your custom prices and settings into your account; values you already set in Exilence CE are kept, and importing again adds nothing twice. **Skip** never asks again; **Settings → Advanced → Import from Exilence CE** runs it any time. Quit Exilence CE first; if anything fails, nothing changes. Imported snapshots stay on this computer and are never shown to Group members.
- A short tour shows the main parts of the app after your first login; **Re-take tour** in the support panel (the question mark in the title bar) runs it again, and after the first tour this changelog opens once by itself. The support panel also offers **What's new** (this changelog), **Feature request** and **Bug report** (on the Exilence CE GitHub; the bug report asks for the Diagnostics bundle), Discord, **Support us** and **Credits**. Links in What's new and Credits open in your browser.
